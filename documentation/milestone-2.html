Morelia Media LLC Portfolio

Content Strategy

My website will showcase my work through Morelia Media LLC, including website design, social media content, and Square Online setup. Each main heading will have its own page. The design will use large image and content blocks inspired by GitHub to make my portfolio easy to explore.

Navigation

A navigation bar will connect the Welcome, About Me, Portfolio, Pricing, Contact, and Social Handles pages. On mobile devices, it will become a hamburger menu to save space. Navigation will remain consistent across the site so visitors can easily move between pages.

Welcome

This page will include a brief introduction that clearly states my name and role as a website designer. I will include a large photo or visual that represents my work, although I have not selected the image yet. A prominent button will invite visitors to explore my portfolio.

About Me

This page will detail my professional history, core skills, and professional highlights. It will explain how my background connects to my current work in website design and digital media. The tone will be personable and casual, allowing visitors to get a sense of my personality and how I work. I want to connect with individuals and organizations whose communication style and goals are a good fit for mine.

Portfolio

This page will showcase examples of my work in three categories: Social Media, Websites, and Square Online. Each category will feature large visual blocks with project previews and short descriptions. Project descriptions will explain what the client needed, what I contributed, and how the finished work supports their business.

Social Media

This section will feature content ideas, simple campaign concepts, and visuals that help local businesses show up consistently online. Examples will include Facebook business pages I have created and videos made for Instagram, Facebook, X, and TikTok. Each example will briefly explain the purpose of the content, such as introducing a business, promoting a service, or encouraging customers to get involved.

Websites

This section will showcase clean, mobile-friendly websites that explain what a business does, build trust, and make it easy for customers to make contact. I will include screenshots or previews of websites I have created, along with links to view them when available. Short descriptions will explain my role in each project and highlight features that address the client’s needs.

Square

This section will showcase my work setting up Square Online stores that clearly display products and make the purchasing process easy to understand. Examples will highlight storefront organization, product presentation, and the steps customers take to place an order. I will also describe the hands-on teaching and coaching I offer to help business owners manage their stores after setup.

Pricing

This page will list prices for selected services and explain what each service includes. Services will be organized into clear categories so visitors can easily compare their options. For projects that require a custom quote, visitors will be encouraged to contact me to discuss their needs.

Contact

This page will feature an inbox or envelope graphic alongside my professional email address. It will also include a scheduling button that opens a calendar with available times for an in-person meeting. The layout will make it easy for potential clients to reach out and start a conversation about their projects.

Morelia Media Social Media Handles

This page will invite visitors to follow my work and stay updated on my projects. It will include links to my GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook profiles. Each profile will have a clearly labeled icon or button so visitors can easily find the platform they prefer.

Wireframes

My website will be responsive, with layouts designed for both desktop and mobile screens. The black-and-white wireframes show the placement of navigation, headings, images, project previews, and contact buttons. On desktop, portfolio images and descriptions will appear in large blocks with room for side-by-side content. On mobile, these blocks will stack vertically as visitors scroll.

The Welcome and Contact pages will use centered cards inspired by GitHub’s sign-in layout. Photos, project images, prices, and other unfinished details will remain placeholders until the final content is selected.
