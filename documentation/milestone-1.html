Morelia Media LLC Portfolio

Purpose for this website:

The purpose of this website is to show local NM buisness owners a portfolio of Morelia Media LLC digital media.

Morelia Media LLC has deployed two websites and created social media content for two Albuquerque companies .

Audience

Buisness owners in Albuquerque and Santa Fe NM who need help establishing an online presence for their company.

Goal

The goal is to attract freelance clients by giving them visibility of previous projects.

In addition, it will serve as a gallery to showcase the companies work.

Persona

Name:Maria Sandoval

Role: Soccer League Owner

Technology being used: iphone 18pro

Behaviours and attitudes: Enthusiatic to create social media content to promote her brand

Frustrations they face: There is competition from another soccer league that appears to be doing very well on social media. She does not know where to start in terms of promoting her brands soccer league on social media. There are many social media platforms and there is a barrier in understanding which is the best and what content is the best to post. They would like video on two topics:

    1. The first game is free for those seeking to join the league.
    2. The league is also recruiting referees.

User story

The user is trying to assess if Morelia Media LLC can provide either social media videos or design websites by looking at their portfolio of past work.
